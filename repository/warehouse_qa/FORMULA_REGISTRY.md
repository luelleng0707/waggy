# Ω8 Warehouse QA Formula Registry

## QA-901 — Schema Validation
- Checks required columns, ID integrity, evidence field presence, and URL format.

## QA-902 — Foreign Keys
- Validates cross-dataset relationship integrity across biological graph layers.

## QA-903 — Duplicate Detection
- Detects duplicated IDs, duplicated concepts, and duplicated claims.

## QA-904 — Evidence Validation
- Validates quote/paper/year/study completeness and suspicious quote reuse.

## QA-905 — Unit Validation
- Detects missing/unknown units, missing basis, and mixed unit systems.

## QA-906 — Ontology & Consistency
- Flags orphan concepts and contradictory scientific claims.

## QA-907 — Coverage Analysis
- Computes condition-path coverage from objectives through products.

## QA-908 — Publication Readiness
- Computes deterministic publication score and readiness summary.
